# Diligence Checklist

Before relying on a company profile, review:

1. Reporting period and fiscal year.
2. Revenue definition and whether it is GAAP revenue, net sales, or another measure.
3. Comparability of periods.
4. Gross profit and margin evidence.
5. Cash balance and burn assumptions.
6. Capital raised and valuation assumptions.
7. Customer concentration and retention evidence.
8. Source provenance for every material input.
9. Missing evidence surfaced by the analysis engine.
10. Filing date versus fiscal-period end date.

VentureLens analysis is intended to organize evidence, not replace primary-source diligence.
