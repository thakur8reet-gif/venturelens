# Feature Engineering v1.0

## Families

### Growth
Revenue, ARR, revenue growth, ARR growth, revenue CAGR, growth acceleration, growth volatility, ARR/revenue.

### Unit economics
Gross margin, CAC, LTV, LTV/CAC, CAC payback, gross revenue retention, net revenue retention, customer churn.

### Capital efficiency
Cash balance, monthly gross burn, monthly net burn, runway, net burn multiple, revenue/burn, ARR/capital raised, cumulative capital raised.

### Financial quality
EBITDA margin, EBITDA growth, FCF margin, FCF conversion, revenue concentration, margin trend, working-capital intensity, capex intensity.

### Market
TAM, SAM, SOM, market growth, market maturity, competitive intensity, regulatory risk, business model, recurring-revenue percentage.

### Valuation
Pre-money, post-money, dilution, revenue multiple, ARR multiple, EV/revenue, EV/ARR, valuation/growth, comparable median multiple, premium/discount to comps.

### Fund fit
Sector, geography, stage, cheque range, ownership target, minimum growth, portfolio overlap, concentration impact, mandate compliance.

### Risk
Market, financial, liquidity, customer, competitive, regulatory, execution, valuation dimensions.

## Calculation rules

- Division by zero returns unavailable with a reason.
- Insufficient history returns unavailable rather than fabricated values.
- Business-model prerequisites are enforced.
- Raw facts retain period and source.
- Derived features retain formula and input references.
- Thresholds are configurable by mandate and peer context.
