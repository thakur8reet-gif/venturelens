# Recommendation Model v1.0

VentureLens prioritizes companies for analyst review. It does not claim to predict investment returns or investment outcomes.

## Dimensions

Growth, Unit Economics, Capital Efficiency, Financial Quality, Market, Valuation, Fund Fit, Risk.

Each dimension is independently scored 0-100 when sufficient evidence exists.

## Evidence states

- reported
- calculated
- estimated
- company_claim
- unavailable

## Workflow states

- strong_fit
- review
- insufficient_evidence
- mandate_mismatch

These are analytical workflow states, not investment advice.

## Explainability

Every assessment records positive and negative contributors plus missing evidence.
