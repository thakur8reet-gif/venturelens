# VentureLens Financial Metrics

Core metrics are derived from reported inputs where enough evidence exists.

- Revenue growth: current revenue / prior revenue - 1
- Revenue CAGR: annualized growth between comparable periods
- Gross margin: gross profit / revenue
- EBITDA margin: EBITDA / revenue
- FCF margin: free cash flow / revenue
- ARR multiple: post-money valuation / ARR
- Dilution: new investment / post-money valuation
- Runway: cash / monthly net burn
- Burn multiple: annualized net burn / net new ARR

Unavailable inputs remain explicitly unavailable rather than being replaced with guessed values.
